About SmartTalkAI

SmartTalkAI is an AI-driven English Speaking Platform designed to help learners practice, evaluate, and improve their communication skills through real-time AI feedback.

Our mission is to make English fluency achievable for everyone — from students preparing for exams to professionals polishing their speaking confidence.

Powered by advanced speech analysis, tone detection, and machine learning, SmartTalkAI personalizes feedback to help you grow step by step.
